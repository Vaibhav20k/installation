## Scope

Whole app shell plus the two phone-first anchors: Dashboard (`/`) and My day (`/my-day`). Mode: Operate. Phone first for every role; desktop is the secondary layout.

## Audience and job

Field crew log time, progress and blockers on site; managers approve and watch conflicts on the move. Success: the next action for the person holding the phone is one thumb-tap away, and every figure opens its records.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The fittings catalogue. Every visit, task and person is a numbered plate with a part code, a spec table and red numbered callouts that point at what to do next. Refuses the field-service default of white cards, blue accent and icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Cool satin plate stock on a pale steel ground, graphite ink, brushed-steel hairlines, one signal red reserved for callouts and blocking states. Archivo for all text, part codes and figures in a mono with tabular numerals. Sharp 4px plate corners, no pills. States are stamps (CONFIRMED, HELD, VOID) printed onto the plate, never colored dots.

STORY: The holder sees their plate first, understands its state from the stamp, and acts through the numbered callouts. Managers read figures as a spec table whose rows open the records behind them.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: compact top strip with the Magppie wordmark and the plate's part code; one full-width plate (code, title, site, stamp) with callouts 1-2-3 as large rows; a graphite bottom bar of five sections inside the safe area. Desktop: an index rail with each section's open count beside the same plates.

FORM: Hardware maker's catalogue, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key f2031f1b. Raises: stamps from the ticket wallet, extent counts from the tab board, rank by weight from the timetable, real numeric density from datamatics. Signature interaction: a stamp presses onto the plate when an action succeeds; quantity steppers press like hardware buttons.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
